## 2. THE FIVE CARDINAL RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE REMNANT

### I. Cooperative Circumnavigation Arbitration
The platform relies on five simultaneous circumnavigation engines:
1. **INS-RLG** (Ring Laser Gyroscope Relativistic Inertial)
2. **STELL-EPHEM** (Exo-atmospheric Celestial Ephemeris Star Tracker)
3. **MAG-GRAV** (Lithospheric Geomagnetic & Gravimetric Gradient Lock)
4. **TERCOM-SAR** (Topographic Synthetic Aperture Radar Contour Lock)
5. **DISSIM-SWARM** (Cooperative Ghost/Swarm Relative Vectoring)

**Remnant Mandate:** Prevent sensory divergence. If spoofing or electronic attack contaminates one or two circumnavigation vectors, the Remnant employs a dynamic Covariance Bounding Algorithm to isolate the contaminated system, purge its weights, and re-stabilize the platform's planetary vector across the remaining cooperative nodes.

### II. Dynamic Entropy Detection & Elimination
Hostile electronic warfare floods radar apertures with synthetic noise, decoys, and false target echoes designed to maximize tactical confusion (high Shannon entropy).

**Remnant Mandate:** 
- Constantly monitor ambient spatial entropy:
  $$H(S) = - \sum_{i=1}^n P(s_i) \log_2 P(s_i)$$
- When entropy exceeds threshold ($H > H_{\text{crit}}$), the Remnant discharges an **Elimination Wavefront**—an active cognitive anti-entropy filter that cross-references phase coherence, doppler micro-shifts, and kinetic reality.
- Annihilate phantom decoys from the targeting matrix, leaving only verified physical assets.

### III. Active Dissimulation Stewardship (Ghost Projection)
While eliminating enemy decoys, the robot must also prevent itself from being targeted.

**Remnant Mandate:** 
- Synthesize dynamic false radar cross-sections (RCS) along divergent trajectory vectors.
- Manipulate RF emission signatures so hostile tracking radars acquire "dissimulated ghost nodes" while the physical robot circumnavigates silently along the optimal tactical vector.

### IV. Allegiance & Kinetic Threat Resolution
Combat theaters feature multi-domain actors: allied escorts, civil satellites, unknown bogeys, and lethal hypersonic threats.

**Remnant Mandate:** 
- Maintain continuous IFF Mode-5 and optical/spectral verification.
- Prohibit collateral engagement against friendly and neutral trajectories.
- Compute multi-target lead fire solutions, intercept triangles, and Time-To-Impact (TTI) vectors with millisecond certainty.

### V. Blazingly Fast Telemetry Crystallization (System Dumps)
Should the platform face catastrophic kinetic destruction or mission extraction, no computational insight must be lost.

**Remnant Mandate:**
- Maintain an ultra-optimized circular ring-buffer operating at sub-millisecond latencies.
- Stream and dump high-fidelity system states (entropy vectors, circumnav consensus weights, target track matrices) into non-volatile optical registers.
- Provide human researchers with exhaustive 120-second engagement captures and 300-second strategic circumnavigation trajectories.
